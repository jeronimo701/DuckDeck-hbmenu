# Native dependencies

DuckDeck retains upstream hbmenu ISC notices. Built with libnx 4.12.0, deko3d 0.5.0, libconfig 1.7.2, FreeType 2.13.3, libjpeg-turbo 2.1.2 and PhysFS 3.2.0, plus the toolchain/runtime dependencies selected by devkitPro pkg-config. Installed licence notices are preserved in notices/.

Upstream source and package build recipes: https://github.com/devkitPro/pacman-packages . Component source repositories: https://github.com/switchbrew/libnx , https://github.com/devkitPro/deko3d , https://github.com/hyperrealm/libconfig/tree/v1.7.2 , https://gitlab.freedesktop.org/freetype/freetype , https://github.com/libjpeg-turbo/libjpeg-turbo , https://github.com/icculus/physfs . Modified hbmenu source and its build instructions are in this repository.
